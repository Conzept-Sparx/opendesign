id: brief
title: Brief analysis
description: Analyse a design brief and turn it into opendesign/brief.md with problem, goals, constraints, assumptions and open questions. Use when the designer shares a brief, client email, RFP, meeting notes or a rough idea.
---
**Goal:** a brief everyone agrees on, with its assumptions exposed.

1. Read whatever the designer provides (pasted text, files, links) and any existing `opendesign/brief.md`.
2. Brainstorm with the designer before writing. Ask the 3–5 questions that most change the design, for example: who exactly is the user, what does success look like, what can't change, what already exists, what's the deadline. Ask them together in one message, not one at a time. If the designer says "just draft it", draft and mark gaps.
3. Write `opendesign/brief.md`:
   - **Problem** in one paragraph from the user's point of view.
   - **Business goals** (BG1…), measurable where possible.
   - **Product context, Constraints, Success looks like.**
   - **Assumptions**: every claim the brief takes for granted, tagged `[assumed]`.
   - **Open questions** for the client.
4. Critique the brief briefly: contradictions, missing users, goals that conflict, scope that looks too large. Put these in Open questions, not in a separate essay.
5. Recommend `research` if most assumptions are untested, otherwise `users`.
